Jadu AR Inc. is an augmented reality company creating immersive mobile games powered by custom technology making high fidelity spatial gameplay accessible on mobile devices.

The studio has released two titles: Jadu: Fight in AR, a real-time PvP game, and PANIC AR, a story-driven wave shooter. PANIC, currently in development, features hundreds of concurrently simulated enemies, 3D projectiles, and advanced post processing, delivering a level of immersion not previously possible on mobile.

Jadu’s generative authoring and scene understanding tools enable character-driven scenarios responsive to player actions and physical context. Both games have garnered strong enthusiasm from players, with a 4.9-star average rating across 400,000 installs.

Jadu AR Inc. was founded in 2019 with a fascination for what virtual characters interacting with physical spaces can do for culture. The company rose to prominence when their AR enabled Web3 assets did $30M worth of secondary trading. Over the years the team has collaborated with Elton John, GRIMES, Lewis Hamilton, Lil Nas X, Michael Bay, Serena Williams & Snoop Dogg.
Jadu has raised $42M from investors including Bain, General Catalyst, LG Tech Ventures and Com2Us.
